# ROI Calculator — Mailprotector

**Built by:** Jennifer Ugarte | Ugarte Consulting

## What It Does
A business-value ROI calculator built for a cybersecurity company 
that needed a simple way to communicate their value proposition to 
prospective clients in business terms rather than technical terms.

## Why I Built It
After watching a LinkedIn interview where the company struggled to 
articulate their ROI in non-technical language, I built this as a 
gift and sent it to them. They loved it.

## Tech Stack
- HTML / JavaScript
- Deployed on Netlify
